## Decisions and Outcomes

- **Research Question 4 was selected as the team's research question.**

- The project will focus on identifying **potentially risky changes**, rather than attempting to conclusively classify artifacts as malicious or safe.

- The exact threat model and definition of "risky behavior" will be established as part of the project.

- The scope will include more than malicious URLs or scripts and may consider command execution, file access, network access, credential references, and other relevant behaviors.

- The team will deliberately constrain the scope to something achievable within the semester.

- GitHub will be used for source control and project/backlog management.

- The backlog will use the following hierarchy:

  **Epic → Story → Task Checklist**

  - Stories will be GitHub sub-issues.
  - Tasks will be checklist items contained within stories rather than separate issues.

- The existing Markdown backlog will serve as the initial working draft and will be reviewed and refined before being entered into GitHub.

- Story points and individual assignments will be added after the backlog content has been finalized.

- Team members may volunteer for stories of particular interest, with work distributed fairly across the group.

- Team roles may be rotated between sprints to provide broader experience.

- Discord will be used for ongoing project discussions and coordination.

- The team will seek instructor clarification before deciding whether the backlog must cover only Sprint 1 or all project sprints.
