# Appendix C Validation scope and status

The saved run generated 30 directed review pairs across six strata and 15 relationship review pairs across three strata, using seed 580. The samples include supported, uncertain, coverage-limited, and unflagged cases. Their purpose is to inspect pipeline decisions, including omissions, rather than estimate population prevalence.

Manual judgments distinguish relatedness, direction, extraction rules, evidence changes, and the eleven defined RIs. An RI false positive is an unsupported reported indicator; an RI false negative is a manually supported in-scope indicator absent from the scanner's supported set. Retained behavior, equivalent syntax, and observations outside RI-001 through RI-011 do not automatically constitute missed indicators.

The current saved summary contains no completed independent validation metrics. Precision, recall, and agreement therefore remain unreported. Detailed annotation instructions and field definitions are retained in the repository resources listed in Appendix E rather than reproduced here.
