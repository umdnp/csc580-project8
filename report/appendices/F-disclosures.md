# Appendix F Use of AI in development and report preparation

Project Group 8 used AI assistance during tool and pipeline development and in preparing this report. Development assistance included discussing implementation approaches, drafting and revising code, troubleshooting errors, and supporting documentation and verification work. These activities contributed to the components integrated into the end-to-end pipeline.

For the report, AI assistance supported organizing material, drafting and revising prose, condensing earlier documentation, and checking consistency between the narrative and saved outputs. It also supported formatting and rebuilding the report. The numerical findings come from the saved pipeline artifacts; AI-generated explanations are not experimental measurements.

Project Group 8 remains responsible for the implementation, interpretation, and final submitted text. Generated code and prose require checking against project requirements, source evidence, and reproducible outputs. AI assistance does not establish the correctness of inferred relationships or reported indicators, and it does not resolve the validation limitations stated in the main report.
