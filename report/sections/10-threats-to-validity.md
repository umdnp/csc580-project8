# Threats to validity

**Construct validity.** The eleven RIs define selected observable security-sensitive changes, not every security risk. Regular-expression rules and evidence extraction can mistake examples, warnings, or equivalent commands for behavior changes and may miss unfamiliar syntax, indirect operations, or instructions expressed only in prose. A larger number of matches is not automatically a higher level of risk. A complete evidence record means a recognized construct was recovered; it does not establish the behavior's runtime effect, safety, or intent.

**Relatedness and historical order.** Identical names can group unrelated skills; renamed relatives can be missed. Token-shingle containment and Jaccard support relatedness but do not prove copying, and similarity clusters do not identify verified original authors or sources. GitSkills does not supply complete historical contents. Commit dates refer to observations at current file paths; equivalent-peer chronology, repository-creation boundaries, and containment extend coverage but remain indirect. **566 of 2,157** directed comparisons use containment as their basis, including **131 of 340** comparisons with supported RIs. Their inferred order needs particular caution.

**Missing evidence.** Some sibling files have no stored text, and historical enrichment is uneven. The three trials report **211 comparisons with incomplete evidence coverage**, including **81** that also have supported indicators. A supported observation can still have complete local evidence when another part of its comparison is missing. Even comparisons marked complete may contain behavior outside the rules. Unknown sibling state is not equivalent to an empty or unchanged bundle.

**Sampling and generalizability.** All three trial samples were stratified at 20/55/20/4/1 families by artifact-count group and required at least one usable primary directed relationship. Larger families were deliberately oversampled; candidate families rejected by the selection screen were not counted as having no findings. The 300 sampled families are non-overlapping but not independent draws from a constant pool because later trials exclude prior selections. Neither the **21.0% family-level rate** nor the **15.8% comparison-level rate** estimates prevalence among all GitSkills families. Family size measures artifact occurrences, not independent authors or historical modifications.

**Dependence and conclusion validity.** Multiple directed comparisons may share artifacts or repeat the same underlying evidence. For example, `mcp-builder` accounts for 133 of the 191 supported-indicator comparisons in Trial 3. Pair-level counts are therefore not independent incidents. The study analyzes primary predecessor edges only; additional directed comparisons could contain distinct observations that are not included. Trial-to-trial comparisons and dominant-family exclusions show sensitivity, but do not substitute for a population-weighted study or a matched all-edge robustness analysis.

**Validation and reproducibility.** The three trials generated 90 directed and 45 relationship-review cases, but their human adjudication is in progress. Scanner precision, recall, and relationship agreement are not established by the automated summaries or by AI-assisted draft notes. The pipeline preserves manifests, data identities, code revision, rules, seeds, and case checksums; numeric artifact IDs remain local to a particular DuckDB build. The saved manifests still mark approval as provisional. Reproducibility safeguards limit accidental changes but do not eliminate selection bias or detection error.

The final conclusion must distinguish supported automated indicators from validated cases, and reported uncertainty from an actual absence of evidence. The full supporting discussion is maintained in `THREATS_TO_VALIDITY.md` and the RDRs.
